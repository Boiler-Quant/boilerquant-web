A **market maker** is a firm that continuously quotes two prices for an asset: a
*bid* (what they'll pay to buy) and an *ask* (what they'll charge to sell). The gap
between them is the **spread**, and capturing it is how market makers earn money.

## Why does the spread exist?

The market maker takes on risk every time they trade: the price might move against
them before they can offset the position. The spread compensates them for that risk
and for the cost of being available to trade at all times.

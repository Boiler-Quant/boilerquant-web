## Summary

The team screened several hundred equity pairs for cointegration, built a
z-score entry/exit model, and backtested it over five years of daily data.

## Results

The strategy was profitable before costs and roughly break-even after realistic
transaction costs, which turned into a useful lesson about where the edge
actually goes. Slides from the final presentation are available on request.
